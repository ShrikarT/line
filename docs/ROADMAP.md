# Line roadmap

## Wave 2 submission

Freeze a compiling twelve-circuit prototype, publish the source, retain finalized
Preprod receipt/output evidence, and deliver a clear deck and recorded user flow.
The current settlement profile pays valueless test tokens to two first-party
merchant wallets. No production business is required for this milestone.

## Organization-funded pilot

Complete the separate wallet-owned facility lifecycle: deployment and all keys,
existing-asset deposit, allowance, both supplier payouts, replenishment,
withdrawal and exact interrupted-request recovery. Verify actual Lace proving
and deploy a persistent supplier service with funded independent custody.

The customer hypothesis is enterprise teams letting agents buy APIs and compute.
Validate one real purchasing task, budget-owner need, supplier acceptance and
willingness to pay before expanding scope. No interviews, pilots or revenue are
currently established.

## Stronger privacy

Keep private limits and openings out of public application views. Treat public
amounts, recipients, reserve changes and timing as observable history. Verify
any redesigned settlement rail against full-history inference before promising
complete balance privacy; native NIGHT is transparent. A shielded test token
alone does not establish genuine backing or redemption.

## Broader credit platform

Pursue shared credit, multiple borrowers, independent capital providers and
additional rails after the organization-funded path and customer evidence are
established. Name who funds, repays, operates custody and bears losses. Prove
asset movement and reconciliation separately from accounting transitions.
