**Verdict:** Line has a substantial, tested Compact accounting and authorization implementation. Real local proofs now work for all twelve circuits and pass separate cryptographic verification. It still does not satisfy the complete privacy, independent custody, network execution, funded settlement or customer-demand gates. The Wave 2 privacy-demo gate remains unmet.

## New architecture findings and authorized direction change

The [twelve-circuit private-book pool](PRIVATE_POOL_2026-10-11.md) passes 39 generated operations and 44 rejection controls with six independently keyed actor processes. It does not pass historical privacy: a public 15-unit purchase plus 1-unit fee reveals a known-zero sole borrower's 16-unit debt, and a recognizable one-borrower historic membership root remains usable after another borrower joins. Full proving artifacts exist. After earlier proving timeouts, a sequential, ephemeral-role run verified eight contract calls across five circuit names, including both two-phase openings and one draw, and Rust rejected a corrupted draw proof. The run stopped on a Docker cleanup timeout before the second draw/repayment/closure. Its published checkpoint is incomplete; canonical all-twelve proof results do not establish experimental pool coverage.

The owner authorized redesign of public amounts and reserve deltas on 11 October. [The concrete next-version mapping](PRIVACY_REDESIGN_2026-10-11.md) preserves exact accounting, independent roles and all eighteen requirements. It requires a supported private reserve/prover boundary, current-root concurrency/recovery and explicit migration; it is not a privacy pass or canonical change. Enterprise teams whose agents buy APIs/compute are the accepted lead customer hypothesis, with no existing contacts or demand evidence.

Published pool/audit commit `b1b400e` passed [GitHub CI](https://github.com/ShrikarT/line/actions/runs/38110884610): 462 tests / 60 suites, 20 browser tests, both compiled architecture experiments, typecheck/build, scans and zero canonical managed drift. The regression run initially exposed a probabilistic test-helper bug: normalized ledger map keys can omit trailing zero bytes. The helper now decodes the declared `Bytes<32>` type, and the refund-recovery test deliberately selects a trailing-zero payment nullifier. The targeted 14 tests and full CI pass; no production recovery bug is inferred from the helper defect.

## Repairs and new evidence

- Linux CI exposed an exact-source fingerprint failure caused by Windows CRLF versus Git LF. Compact, managed output and compiler-wrapper checkout rules now force LF; recovery provenance and public verifier fixtures were regenerated from a freshly validated full release. The pushed `a5cc4f8` run [passed CI](https://github.com/ShrikarT/line/actions/runs/38077327909). This was a portability bug, not a semantic contract change.
- The isolated custody variant preserves all twelve circuit names. The agent prepares a domain/generation/book/expiry/fee/terms-bound consent commitment; the issuer accepts those exact terms without the agent secret. Five independently keyed processes reject missing/altered consent, wrong roles/agent, over-limit and replayed draws, stolen redemption and redemption replay. This is feasibility, not canonical integration, production isolation or legal-borrower authority.
- [Real proof evidence](proof-2026-10-11-evidence/verification.json) records 19 contract proofs across all twelve canonical circuits and ten negative checks. The supported HTTP prover and separate Rust ledger 8.1.3 verifier accept the valid proofs. Three decodable corrupted draw proofs reject in Rust. The lifecycle includes both merchants, repayment-reference replay after a new draw, actual wall-clock expiry, compensation and zero-debt closure. Signing keys and openings still share one orchestrator; the loopback prover receives private preimages.
- Earlier remediation includes stable payment-reference nullifiers, authenticated zero-debt closure, ledger-time expiry, exact issuer fee policy, pending/earned fee accounting, note-bound compensation, encrypted operation journals, process-death recovery and domain-bound private books. These repairs close specific local defects; they do not close the blockers above.

## Execution order

Midnight's [official event page](https://midnight.network/hackathon/buildathon), checked 11 October, lists Wave 2 building September 27–October 17 and judging October 17–27. It emphasizes iterative progress across waves. The exact AKINDO cutoff time/timezone and detailed scoring rubric were not available from the public page extraction; these dates are not a verified submission timestamp. Freeze the submitted commit, reproduce the demo and attach evidence for each claimed capability.

The primary [Mandate repository](https://github.com/tomiin/midnight-mandate) explicitly targets Wave 2 agent spending authorization. It documents public caps, running totals, amounts and linkable pseudonyms, and reports a Preprod demo; this audit has not independently verified its network receipts. This is direct thematic overlap. Line's proposed credit, merchant claims and complete settlement distinction must be demonstrated; calling the category novel or adding more console polish will not establish it. This is a strategic inference, not a verified judging outcome.

1. Prove and integrate the compatible privacy/custody architecture. Establish the actual observer model and borrower authority before presenting a confidential credit product.
2. Complete fee-funded network proving, submission, finality and recovery with reproducible receipts. Keep local proof evidence clearly labeled.
3. Authenticate deposits, merchant payments, repayments and refunds against an actual asset rail and accountable counterparties.
4. Pursue customer discovery alongside engineering. Choose a lead segment from observed transactions and buying authority, rather than the appeal of an autonomous-agent narrative.
5. Build the judging demonstration around the resulting evidence: two independent merchants, valid/declined/replayed purchases, recovery, a complete privacy transcript and a separately reconciled payment. Keep the full product requirements in scope; a deadline does not establish readiness.
