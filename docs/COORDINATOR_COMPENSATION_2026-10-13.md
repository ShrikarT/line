## Implemented modes and retained state

| Mode | Generated sequence | Saved private state |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — expiry | Issuer expires the original Merchant A note after explicit preview time advancement | Original note metadata opening/salt and reserve opening; source supplies all required typed witnesses; debt stays unchanged |
| 1 — allocation | Agent allocates the partially repaid Merchant A claim; issuer allocates an original-generation Merchant B claim after reopening; agent allocates a current-generation zero-cash claim | Exact note identity/generation/nonce/salt, current book/path/salt, new book salt and immutable refund opening keyed by `D` |
| 2 — acknowledgment | Issuer acknowledges 7-unit partial cash, 17-unit earlier-generation cash and zero obligation from synthetic owner records | Allocated refund preimage/salt, exact reference/amount/deadline and source-bound approval; no accidental book rotation |

Allocation updates debt only for a current-generation note. Older-generation allocation preserves current debt and owes the full original cost. Positive cash retains the full original reserve budget; acknowledgment moves that budget into reported holds. Zero cash releases its obligation hold, and its acknowledgment moves no holds. These are source-defined accounting consequences, not proof of money movement.

Partial and zero refund acknowledgments occur after later draws change the current book salt. The worker uses the original saved refund-opening salt, rather than the latest book salt. Commitment-keyed original note/refund records survive newer supplier selections and changed-terms reopening.

All 23 recovered actions in this fixture are deliberately interrupted after authorization intent persistence, owner approval, candidate publication and confirmation. Restart restores the saved inputs, signature/nonce and semantic request. Exact completed retries pass and substituted requests reject. Four pending draws also reject preview-clock changes. Repayment references cannot acknowledge refunds, and refund references—including zero-obligation references—cannot be credited as repayments. Expired/spent/redeemed and foreign-supplier claims reject without leaving a new intent.

## Owner-local receipt authority

`OwnerReceiptRegistry` uses the existing encrypted checkpoint store under an immutable custody/domain/beneficiary/denomination binding. Its local admission method validates exact record fields and immutable reference/purpose/amount/note associations. There is no remote admission endpoint. Returned records are detached; wrong scope/password and record reassignment reject. The fixture closes and reopens the registry before using a saved refund record.

`createCompensationPolicy` requires explicit owner-selected modes. Allocations require the configured issuer or the actual agent's own book. Refund acknowledgment requires the issuer, a matching registry authority binding, exact note/reference/amount/beneficiary/domain/denomination and deadline. Repayment uses admitted records with a distinct purpose. Existing purchase/lifecycle policies are not automatically broadened.

Admission is an **owner assertion**. The fixture admits `synthetic-fixture` records denominated in `synthetic-units`; a zero record acknowledges absence of a cash obligation, not a fabricated zero-value transfer. The registry has no bank/token verifier, standard denomination/recipient interpretation, external reversal/dispute reconciliation or anti-rollback certification. Actual receipt admission and independent operation/CLI integration remain work. Possession of a software-store password is not institutional or hardware custody.
