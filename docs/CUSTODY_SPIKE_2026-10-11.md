# Independent-key opening feasibility - 11 October 2026

The experiment in [custody-spike.mjs](../scripts/custody-spike.mjs) compiles a source-derived variant of the complete twelve-circuit contract with pinned compiler 0.31.1. It changes opening into two phases of `openLine`: agent request (`action=0`), then issuer acceptance (`action=1`). All twelve exported circuit names and the other eleven parameter signatures must match canonical metadata. The experimental opening has an additional public action parameter and is not ABI-compatible with canonical opening. It does not modify or deploy the canonical contract.

Run `npm run custody:spike`. Experimental source, generated bindings and a fresh report are retained in a new ignored `.compact-keys/custody-spike-*` directory. [Recorded evidence](custody-2026-10-11-evidence/feasibility.json) contains no signing secrets, private openings or witness outputs.

Five separate child processes represent issuer, agent, Merchant A, Merchant B and an unauthorized second agent. Each process generates its own secret and returns only its public identity/key. The coordinator has no signing-secret witness implementations; actor IPC rejects supplied `callerSecret` or `agentSecret` values. The issuer and merchants throw if the generated contract asks them for an agent secret. Processes share public contract state and only the non-signing opening/quote/note data needed for their authorized operations. The same machine and coordinator remain trusted orchestration for this experiment; OS processes do not establish independent operators or a production security boundary.

Observed results:

- The agent prepares a request bound to its secret-derived identity, domain, next generation, initial book commitment, expiry, fee policy and accepted-terms digest. This records consent without opening the facility or requiring an issuer key. Duplicate preparation rejects.
- Issuer acceptance without a request rejects. Altered limit, flat fee or terms reject. The issuer opens the requested 40-unit line using the agent's non-secret identity, with no agent-secret witness call. The resulting commitment equals the independently computed domain-bound opening.
- The agent cannot open a line as issuer; issuer and merchants cannot draw with an agent secret they do not possess; a different agent holding its own secret cannot draw against the enrolled identity.
- The enrolled agent draws 25. Replay/stale opening and a further 20-unit over-limit draw reject. The generated commitment matches the exact 25-unit debt transition.
- Merchant B cannot redeem Merchant A's note; Merchant A redeems it once and replay rejects.
- Agent repayment acknowledgment rejects. Issuer acknowledgment of 10 evaluation units updates debt from 25 to 15 without consuming an agent-secret witness. It authenticates no cash receipt. Reserve counters remain `T=100`, `E=0`, `R=25`.
- The public singleton history reconstructs the pre-repayment debt of 25 from known zero and the note's public principal/fee. This is a privacy counterexample, not a passing privacy test.

This establishes a compiling route to removing the opening's signing-secret co-witness requirement and binding agent-key consent to the facility terms without a thirteenth entry point or unsupported signature API. It does **not** establish that this key has authority to bind the legal borrower, that the complete terms are meaningful or enforceable, or that independently operated systems retain these custody boundaries. Canonical ABI/runtime/console integration, independently operated encrypted stores, revocation/recovery and borrower authority remain required under PD-02. No private-pool construction, ZK proof, network transaction, payout or customer validation is established by this result. Canonical opening still consumes an agent-secret witness.

Next implementation steps retain the complete product destination: establish accountable borrower authority and complete operating terms; integrate the two-phase opening without importing agent secrets into issuer custody; replace linkable singleton books with a supported multi-borrower construction and verify complete observer transcripts, concurrency, lifecycle and public accounting; prove/independently verify the scoped circuits on supported infrastructure; then authenticate settlement receipts and conduct the customer evidence protocol. The Wave 2 privacy-demo gate remains unmet.
