# Recoverable repayment and bilateral reopening

The private worker now constructs `acknowledgeRepayment` and retains the two-party enrollment packet needed for closed-book reopening. Dispatch covers eleven of twelve names. The Compact source is unchanged; `cancelOrExpireNote` and its three compensation modes remain unintegrated. Native/network proof of the newer lifecycle, real payment authentication and complete observer privacy remain open.

## Repayment authority and custody

The coordinator accepts issuer instructions with exact private amount, payment reference and receipt deadline. It saves the predecessor book/salt, new salt, receipt nonce and selected approval descriptor before the issuer RPC. Confirmation advances debt/epoch/salt and retains the exact receipt record in encrypted financial state. Completed retries must match the original semantic request. Overpayment, empty references, incorrect roles and actual Compact payment-nullifier reuse reject before a new authorization intent. The reference nullifier is domain-bound and independent of book epoch/generation, so closing/reopening cannot make the same reference spendable again.

`approval-owner-lifecycle-policy.mjs` is a separately selected `lifecycleMode: 'owner-records'` policy. The issuer must pin receipt records matching reference, amount, domain, beneficiary identity and the configured denomination. It checks the own book, debt and deadline. Existing purchase/governance policies still deny repayment; this extension does not silently broaden old stores.

The owner is responsible for authenticating records **before** selecting this policy. The local fixture supplies explicitly synthetic records and `synthetic-units`. Matching an immutable allowlist authenticates neither a bank deposit nor a blockchain token transfer. Denomination matches the owner configuration; Compact does not decode the external facility terms or verify a currency/rail. Owner-record admission, real receipt verification, reconciliation, dispute/reversal handling and capital accounting remain separate work. Debt headroom is not cash replenishment.

An unknown owner receipt or another policy denial can still leave the saved authorization intent unresolved. A generic denial is not proof that the operation was never approved: exact retries are checked against the current clock/policy even when an approval was previously committed. Do not delete such intents or issue fresh nonces automatically. A separately authenticated terminal-resolution procedure remains needed; the preflight correction closes known spent-reference staging, not every denied-operation recovery case.

## Enrollment and reopening

Agent consent retains the exact proposed book, new salt, predecessor book/salt and accepted terms in the encrypted confirmed financial state. Issuer acceptance uses that packet; it cannot independently invent a new salt or substitute a facility. Optional facility instructions include limit, expiry, flat fee, basis points and typed terms. After acceptance, the worker retains the new book and clears the completed enrollment packet. Current public generated books must contain its exact new commitment.

Reopening requires a closed, debt-free predecessor and increments its generation with epoch reset to zero. Both agent and issuer select a pinned terms catalog and bounded facility values. The fixture proposes a different terms commitment, limit, fee and expiry, then obtains both approvals against one shared packet. Original-generation note openings remain archived and independently redeemable. A quote bound to an earlier generation rejects before new authorization, even when that quote was never drawn.

No automatic reconstruction or migration of an old missing enrollment packet is added. Earlier stores whose agent consent was confirmed without retaining that packet cannot use the new issuer-acceptance path without a separately reviewed recovery. Preserve their original encrypted records and public approvals. Existing prepared packets still have their exact saved inputs; compatibility of every historical pending/authorization state is not certified by fresh fixtures.
