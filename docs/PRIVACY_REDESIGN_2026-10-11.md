# Authorized disclosure redesign — 11 October 2026

The project owner explicitly authorized **redesign of public amounts and reserve deltas** on 11 October. The former fixed-disclosure boundary now describes the canonical baseline, not an immutable requirement for the next version. This authorization changes neither the canonical contract nor its disclosure inventory. No implementation or privacy pass is established by this design document.

## Problem the next version must actually solve

The compiled pool removes singleton borrower fields and privately proves membership, but does not protect historical debt. A known-zero sole borrower purchasing 15 with fee 1 owes exactly 16, directly reconstructed from public notes. Recognizable historic roots additionally narrow the candidate population: after two enrollments, a draw can still disclose the earlier one-borrower membership root. More leaves, fresh salts and multiple agents do not resolve these failures.

## Concrete candidate and circuit mapping

Keep exactly the twelve circuit names, authenticated roles, domain separation, exact accounting and merchant-bound single-redemption notes. Change the versioned ABI and private-state schema where needed. Compact `persistentCommit`/`persistentHash` remain the cryptographic primitives; source fingerprints are provenance, not protocol commitments.

| State or transition | Candidate change | Required checks |
|---|---|---|
| Reserve state | Replace the seven public totals `T,E,R,F,P,U,X` with one domain-bound, salted commitment to a private reserve book and monotonic epoch. Every reserve-changing circuit proves the current opening and emits a fresh commitment. | Exact `E+R+F+P+U+X <= T`; Uint widths/overflow; authenticated initialization; different-domain rejection; no public amounts in arguments, queries, outputs or diffs. |
| `fundReserve`, `withdrawUnencumberedReserve`, `withdrawFees` | Issuer supplies private amounts/opening. Prove exact arithmetic and rotate reserve state; bind asset receipts separately. | Funding/withdrawal authority, free-reserve bound, earned-fees-only withdrawal, duplicate receipt prevention. Counter changes remain accounting until an asset rail is verified. |
| `registerMerchant`, `disableMerchant` | Preserve issuer authority and two-merchant isolation. Inventory registry and selected-merchant disclosure rather than claiming anonymity. | No unauthorized enrollment; disabled new offers reject; outstanding legitimate claims retain agreed rights. |
| `openLine` | Preserve agent-prepared, issuer-accepted consent over exact private terms and predecessor. Hide unique pricing and borrower lifecycle metadata where the toolchain permits; use a declared expiry policy. | Issuer never needs agent signing key; altered consent fails; repeated initial issuance and reopening replay fail; named legal borrower separately accepts liability. |
| `postQuote`, `draw` | Keep buyer/merchant/generation/fee-bound quote commitments. Move fee/amount arguments and note metadata into private authenticated openings. Prove exact fee, book headroom and private reserve backing. | Both merchants; wrong buyer/merchant; zero/overflow values; exact rounding; double spend; public transcript and amount-guessing attacks. Merchant knows its own price. |
| `redeemDraw` | Merchant proves the note opening and private amount/fee, consumes one-time nullifier and rotates reserve commitment `E -> R`, `P -> F`. | Stolen/replayed claim rejection; note-state integrity; redemption is distinct from delivery and paid payout. |
| `cancelOrExpireNote` | Prove original note amounts privately. Preserve `E/P -> U`, debt-first compensation and full original hold `U -> X` on authenticated refund report. Consider committing lifecycle/refund predicates too. | Exact expiry cutoff; compensation once; old-generation claim preserves new debt; partial debt/cash cases; public cash-owed/close predicates assessed as potential debt bounds. |
| `acknowledgeRepayment`, `setStatus` | Preserve stable payment-reference nullifier and private book rotation. Keep repayment and reserve cash inflow distinct. Reduce linkable status/zero-debt disclosure where possible. | No reference replay across a later draw; correct borrower allocation; zero-debt closure; default cannot erase debt or merchant claims. |
| Membership | Require the **current** common pool root for spend transitions, or prove another construction prevents deliberate old-population selection. | Historic-root attack rejects; concurrent independently keyed books serialize/rebase safely; stale openings recover without duplicate debt or lost private state. |

The private reserve book creates a custody and availability question: which actor obtains its current opening to prove a draw or redemption? Giving every merchant/agent that opening reveals pool balances to those actors; placing all signing secrets in one reserve service breaks independent custody. Do not call either a solution. Compare an independently operated, explicitly trusted reserve co-prover/capability boundary with shielded asset-native accounting or a supported compositional proof construction. Confirm actual pinned Compact/SDK APIs before choosing. Shared host processes and differently addressed provers are feasibility tools, not independently operated institutions.

## Verification and migration gates

1. Write the precise observer and authorized-information model: issuer, legal borrower, each merchant, public reader, fee payer/relayer, prover and settlement operator. A merchant necessarily knows its own purchase; it must not learn unrelated book facts through shared openings or metadata.
2. Compile the full twelve-circuit candidate with the pinned compiler. Regenerate its own managed assets and fingerprints; never reuse baseline verifier keys. Run lifecycle, arithmetic, role, concurrency, expiry, compensation and recovery attacks.
3. Inspect actual public call inputs/outputs, operation transcripts and ledger diffs. Compare known-zero solo worlds with different protected debts but equivalent authorized public outputs. Test amount dictionaries, fee/deadline fingerprints, root choice, sender/DUST metadata and counterparties' side information. Finite tests cannot certify computational indistinguishability.
4. Generate and verify real proofs in a separate proof-enabled Rust process, including decodable corrupted-proof controls. Then exercise fee-funded network submission, finality and uncertain-confirmation recovery. Local synthetic state is not network evidence.
5. Define a versioned migration with explicit asset/liability reconciliation and old-claim handling. Existing commitments and public histories cannot be made confidential retroactively. Do not silently import historical books into a new privacy claim or overwrite canonical managed bindings.
6. Independently review the construction and operational access/retention model. Authenticate genuine funding, service, payout, repayment and refunds. Preserve all eighteen product requirements; this disclosure change does not establish settlement, underwriting, customer demand or hackathon readiness.

The next implementation decision is the reserve-opening/prover boundary. Full-history privacy remains failed/unproven until the implementation and complete observer views support the promised guarantee.
